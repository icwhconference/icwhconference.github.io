A platform for women shaping the future of healthcare.

ICWH is an annual international platform designed to empower women in healthcare through mentorship, leadership development, career advancement, advocacy, networking, innovation and collaboration. The inaugural edition — ICWH 2026 — will bring together students, healthcare professionals, leaders, innovators, advocates and partners. ICWH will continue as an annual conference with future editions building on this global movement.

Why Attend

Join a global community advancing women's leadership, careers and impact across healthcare disciplines.

01

Mentorship & Empowerment

Connect with mentors and role models who will support professional growth and provide guidance for career progression.

02

Medical Career Advancement

Gain insights and practical advice for clinical careers, research pathways and leadership roles in healthcare.

03

Sustainable Mentorship & Leadership

Engage in sessions that build long-term mentorship networks and sustainable leadership practices.

04

Women’s Health Advocacy

Explore policy, advocacy strategies, and community initiatives to advance women’s health at local and global levels.

05

Innovation & Collaboration

Discover innovations in healthcare delivery, technology, and cross-disciplinary collaboration that improve outcomes for women.

06

Students in Healthcare

A dedicated focus for students to learn, network, and prepare for leadership roles in clinical and public health fields.
