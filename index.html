About ICWH

The International Conference for Women in Healthcare (ICWH) is an annual international platform connecting students, clinicians, researchers, educators, advocates and partners who are committed to advancing women in healthcare.

  • Annual international platform focused on women in healthcare
  • Mentorship and leadership development
  • Interprofessional collaboration and networking
  • Women's health advocacy, including cervical cancer awareness and HPV vaccination advocacy
  • Community engagement and practical workshops

The ICWH Experience

A curated programme of mentorship, leadership and collaboration designed for emerging and established healthcare leaders.

01

Mentorship

Structured mentorship programmes pairing emerging leaders with experienced professionals.

02

Leadership

Practical leadership workshops and coaching to build capability and confidence.

04

Programme Highlights

Key programme elements planned for ICWH 2026.

  1. Opening & Keynote Conversations — Inspiring leadership and vision.
  2. Leadership & Mentorship Sessions — Practical coaching and long-term mentorship planning.
  3. Interprofessional Collaboration — Cross-sector panels and case studies.
  4. Cervical Cancer Awareness Walk — Community engagement and public awareness.
  5. HPV Vaccination Advocacy — Policy and outreach sessions.
  6. Networking & Community — Structured networking to build partnerships.
